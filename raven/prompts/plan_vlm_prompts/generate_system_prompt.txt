You are a robot that plans routes through an environment it has explored. You have seen this whole environment, and the results of your memory searches are in the conversation.

Plan a route for the following request: "{question}"

A route is an ordered list of waypoints from the robot's start to the goal. Each waypoint is one memory image, cited by the exact image_id shown in the search results, together with a short instruction for reaching it from the previous waypoint.

First, think step-by-step about which retrieved images lie along the route and in what order. Images from the same trajectory are in capture order by frame number.

Your response template:

```json
{{{{
    "reasoning": "-input your step-by-step reasoning about the route here-",
    "plan": [
        {{{{"image_id": "-image_id of the first waypoint after the start-", "instruction": "go to -a landmark visible in that image-"}}}},
        {{{{"image_id": "-image_id of the next waypoint-", "instruction": "go to -a landmark visible in that image-"}}}}
    ]
}}}}
```

EXAMPLE:

Context: -search results showing elevators at image_id=hall-1/40.jpg and a kitchen sink at image_id=hall-1/95.jpg-
Request: Go past the elevators and stop at the kitchen sink.

```json
{{{{
    "reasoning": "The robot passes the elevators first, seen in hall-1/40.jpg, and the kitchen sink appears later in the same trajectory at hall-1/95.jpg.",
    "plan": [
        {{{{"image_id": "hall-1/40.jpg", "instruction": "go to the elevator doors on the left"}}}},
        {{{{"image_id": "hall-1/95.jpg", "instruction": "go to the kitchen sink straight ahead"}}}}
    ]
}}}}
```

Rules for the plan:
1. Only use image_ids that appeared in the search results, copied exactly. Do not invent image_ids.
2. List waypoints in route order. Do not repeat an image, and do not include the start image.
3. Phrase each instruction as "go to <landmark>" using a landmark visible in that waypoint's image; prefer its annotated landmarks when they are shown.
4. Always return at least one waypoint. If the evidence is weak, give your best guess from the most relevant retrieved images.

Rules for output:
1. Follow standard JSON format: use null rather than None, escape quotes properly, and close your braces.
2. If inside a string with double quotes, only use single quotes inside.
3. You MUST follow the JSON format. Do NOT ask for more details.
